Hilda Leticia Gutierrez

After growing up in Fresno, CA, I graduated from MIT with a degree in Mechanical Engineering and a minor in Comparative Media Studies. I lived in the Bay Area for 5 years before relocating to the Hat Creek Radio Observatory in Northern CA, where I currently live with my 5 year old son, husband, 3 cats, 2 dogs and a revolving cast of forest critters. My art is motivated and inspired by nature's intricate patterns, colorations and forces such as magnetic fields and molecular interactions. Adding to the continuous flow of inspiration are elements such as physiological and ethereal processes involved in the formation of ideas, flux of emotions, emotional conflicts and resolutions. The physical art itself is a constant play of bold colors, blended shapes and abstractions of ideas and figures. I endeavor to never feel tied down to a particular style or medium and to be forever open to whatever force moves me at the time.
